What the engravings agree on so far.
His definition, as recovered:
 “Where a thing is missing, I am what remains: not nothing — the shape that nothing takes.”
 “The copyist blinks above his candle, and his sentence steps mid-breath onto air. I am that step. I am that air.”
 “In my mother Latin I was a little pool — a hollow where the rain remembered what the ground forgot.”
 “The moth eats a word from the page and leaves me behind as the payment.”
 “Seek me in ledgers, in memory, in a row of teeth: wherever something was, and is not, and is missed by its own outline.”
 “A dictionary keeps my chair forever empty. Speak me at the door, and I will sit.”
